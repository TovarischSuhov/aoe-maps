A collection of dynamic maps for Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition. These are not just terrain and resources: every map carries its own XS script with living mechanics - weather auras, scripted events, conditions that change mid-game. The collection keeps growing, and every map is described in detail below.

FrozenLake

A circular map. At the center sits an autumn-snow island, ringed by water and an icy shore, then a snow road ring, and beyond it a dense winter forest. Players are placed in a circle, and every road leads to the lake.

Frost and thaw. From time to time frozen winds rise over the lake: every unit near the lake - villagers and soldiers alike - starts losing health rapidly. Then a thaw sets in and the shore is safe again. Every weather change is announced with a prominent on-screen notice with sound, so it cannot be missed. Watch the phase before sending villagers to the water or building near the shore.

Lake fauna. Invulnerable penguins roam the snow ring, indestructible beacon outposts mark the shore, and arctic foxes and wolves haunt the forest.

Map sizes. The lake scales with the map size: a small map gets a small lake, a huge one - a large lake. The frost aura always covers the whole lake zone and never reaches the player home areas.

SilentWoods — Night Woods

An open temperate-woods map with hilltop starts and a standard resource set. There is no neutral gold or stone anywhere on the map - instead, cave hills are scattered around: dark dirt caps with a rock and a small pile. Every player gets two gold caves and one stone cave, plus a personal cave by their distant gold mine. Expansion runs through the caves.

Day and night. Every 5 minutes day turns to night: the screen darkens and a pack of six sharp-sighted wolves walks out of every cave. Wolves hunt on their own and do not vanish at dawn - the packs accumulate night over night. Every phase change is announced with an on-screen notice with sound.

Note. Every player in the lobby needs the mod. The maps are experimental: mechanics may change between versions - see the Changelog. Day/night and weather effects require the Map lighting graphics setting (Options → Graphics): without it these effects are not rendered.
